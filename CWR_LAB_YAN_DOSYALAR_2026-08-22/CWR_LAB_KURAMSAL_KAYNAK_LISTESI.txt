Functional analysis: Riesz/vector lattices, L-spaces ve M-spaces, order duals, positive operators, Hahn–Banach, Choquet/conical measures, Banach-space representations, Lipschitz-free / Arens–Eells spaces.

Convexity ve information geometry: zonotopes, convex-order, majorization, Strassen dilation order, support functions, convex Chebyshev radii, polyhedral decision regions, Birkhoff polytope, doubly stochastic matrices.

Markov chains ve contraction: Dobrushin contraction coefficient, ergodicity coefficients, Doeblin-type mixing, Perron–Frobenius theory, primitive matrices, Wielandt exponent, scrambling/SIA matrices, weak ergodicity.

Nonlinear filtering / HMM: Stratonovich filtering viewpoint, nonlinear filter stability, hidden Markov models, Birkhoff/Hilbert projective metric, filter memory, smoother stability, subrectangular stepping matrices, rank-one closure, unique ergodicity.

Projective geometry: Hilbert projective metric, Birkhoff contraction theorem, projective diameter, positive cones, positive matrix products.

Random matrix products: Furstenberg–Kesten type products, Lyapunov exponents, occurrence of contracting words, block contraction, regeneration/renewal ideas.

Probabilistic automata ve computability: Rabin probabilistic automata, cutpoint emptiness, value-1 problem, leaktight automata, Markov monoid, stabilization monoids, prostochastic/profinite completion, Post Correspondence Problem reductions.

Synchronization: Černý/reset words, deterministic synchronization, stochastic synchronization, approximate pair synchronization, rank-one products, probabilistic/weighted automata.

Linear recurrence sequences / number theory: Skolem Problem, Skolem–Mahler–Lech theory, Positivity Problem, Ultimate Positivity, real-root LRS, simple LRS, exponential-polynomial sign problems.

Linear dynamical systems: Kannan–Lipton Orbit Problem, polyhedron/halfspace hitting, matrix-semigroup reachability, switched linear systems.

Reversible stochastic dynamics: detailed balance, self-adjoint Markov operators, common stationary law, real-spectrum dynamics, symmetric stochastic matrices, positive-definite matrix products.

Continuous-time dynamics: reversible CTMC generators, exponential polynomials, Continuous Skolem problem, commensurate decay spectra.

Matrix factorization / symmetric dilation: symmetric doubly stochastic factorization, block dilation, positive mixing shells, Hadamard embeddings, zero-sum tangent-space embeddings.

Control theory tarafı: Pontryagin maximum principle, Bellman dynamic programming, relaxed/switching control, Maslov/tropical Hamiltonian viewpoint.

Differential geometry ve singular systems: Stefan–Sussmann orbit theorem, Hörmander bracket generation, null distributions, Fisher nullspaces, bracket-generated semantic collapse.

Approximation / inverse problems: Kolmogorov widths, Morozov discrepancy principle, Tikhonov regularization, deficiency-calibrated inverse problems.

Information theory: Gács–Körner common information, Blackwell deterministic common core, strong data-processing inequalities, conditional mutual information forgetting.

Duality/category theory tarafı: Stone duality, Priestley duality, Gelfand–Naimark duality, affine duality, locale/frame duality, reflector/coreflector ideas.

2. Laboratuvarda açılan başlıca araştırma programları

Araştırmanın ana tarihsel hücreleri şu şekilde ilerledi:

CW-001 — Blackwell × Le Cam × Riesz/order completion. Blackwell order’ın lattice olmamasından yola çıkıldı; decision-envelope completion, coherent decision systems ve sonunda canonical Dedekind–MacNeille completion ayrıştırıldı. Ana soru: MacNeille completion, Le Cam generalized experiments ve deficiency completion arasındaki ilişki.

CW-002 — Dobrushin × Le Cam × Wald. Markov mixing’den başlangıç state’i hakkındaki tüm future observation tail’ın decision value’suna sharp bound çıkarıldı.

CW-002B — Birkhoff × nonlinear filtering × Le Cam × Wald. Ex-ante Dobrushin bound pathwise/history-dependent hale getirildi.

CW-002C — Perron–Frobenius/Wielandt × Birkhoff. One-step positivity kaldırılıp primitive positive blocks kullanıldı.

CW-002D — Kaijser × subrectangular products × Le Cam. Observation words’ün repeated contraction etkisi future-tail deficiency’ye bağlandı.

CW-002E/F — Rabin × Černý × probabilistic automata × Dobrushin. Weighted pair synchronization’ın general durumda undecidability’si ile strictly-positive/uniformly-scrambling decidability island’ı karşılaştırıldı.

CW-002G — Leaktight automata × support/limit defects. Leak kavramının support Booleanization ile stochastic limit arasındaki noncommutation olduğunu ayırdık.

CW-002H/I — Rote × mixing × zero-margin verification. Exponential mixing altında bile exact stationary threshold’da undecidability yaşayabildiği; nonzero margin’da finite horizon çıktığı görüldü.

CW-002J — LRS × Markov chains. Stationary-coordinate deviations’ın LRS olduğu ve stochastic tangent dimension’ın n−1 olduğu ayrıştırıldı.

CW-002K — reversibility. Reversible single-chain probleminde real spectrum nedeniyle discrete halfspace/hyperplane verification’ın arbitrary dimension’da decidable olduğu çıkarıldı.

CW-002L/M — reversible switching ve alphabet compression. Reversible microsteps ile nonreversible macro-computation, ardından binary positive bistochastic phase coding geliştirildi.

CW-002N — Hadamard tangent stochasticization. Rational symmetric matrix semigroups’un strictly-positive symmetric doubly stochastic Markov dynamics’e exact multiplicative embedding’i kuruldu. Current frontier artık symmetric matrix-semigroup scalar-sign reachability.

Bunların yanında daha erken hücreler de açık: Kolmogorov widths × Lipschitz-free spaces; Kantorovich × Krein–Rutman × Choquet; Morozov × Tikhonov × Le Cam; Čencov/Rao × LAN; Gács–Körner × Blackwell; Strassen × majorization; Pontryagin × Bellman × Maslov; Watanabe × Stefan–Sussmann × Hörmander.

4. Doğrudan incelenen / araştırmada merkezi kullanılan kaynaklar

Bunlar araştırma kararlarımızı doğrudan değiştiren veya theorem/collision audit’te merkezi kullandığımız kaynaklardır.

Lucien Le Cam — “Sufficiency and Approximate Sufficiency” (1964), Annals of Mathematical Statistics 35(4), 1419–1455. General transitions, Riesz-space/statistical-experiment hattının temel kaynaklarından biri.
Erik Torgersen — Comparison of Statistical Experiments (1991). Özellikle generalized decision rules/transitions, vector lattices, generalized experiments, deficiencies, representations/functionals, conical measures, majorization/dilations bölümleri incelendi. Bu kitap CW-001’in ana tarihsel kaynaklarından biri.
Nils Bertschinger & Johannes Rauh — “The Blackwell relation defines no lattice” (2014). Blackwell order’ın genel durumda lattice olmadığını ve explicit counterexample’ları veriyor.
Rami Atar & Ofer Zeitouni — “Exponential stability for nonlinear filtering” (1997). Filter memory length’i Birkhoff contraction coefficient ile bağlıyor; CW-002B’nin historical engine’i.
Rami Atar & Ofer Zeitouni — “Lyapunov Exponents for Finite State Nonlinear Filtering” (1997). Filter sensitivity ve Lyapunov-exponent hattı.
Curtis McDonald & Serdar Yüksel — “Exponential Filter Stability via Dobrushin's Coefficient” (2019). Transition ve observation kernels’in Dobrushin coefficients’ını ortak contraction condition’da kullanıyor; controlled extensions de içeriyor.
Thomas Kaijser — “On Markov chains induced by partitioned transition probability matrices.” Subrectangular stepping-matrix/filtering hattının ana kaynaklarından.
Fred Kochman & Jim Reeds — “A simple proof of Kaijser’s unique ergodicity result for hidden Markov α-chains” (2006). Kaijser condition’ının daha sade/kuvvetli treatment’ı.
Pavel Chigansky & Ramon van Handel — “A complete solution to Blackwell's unique ergodicity problem for hidden Markov chains” (2010). Blackwell’ın 1957 problemine necessary-and-sufficient çözüm; Kaijser/Kochman–Reeds sonuçlarını subsume ediyor.
Boaz Shuval & Ido Tal — “Universal Polarization for Processes with Memory”; ilk arXiv 2018, konferans 2019, genişletilmiş journal version 2025. HMM forgetfulness ve contracting subrectangular-product machinery için kullanıldı.
Nathanaël Fijalkow, Hugo Gimbert, Youssouf Oualhadj — “Deciding the Value 1 Problem of Probabilistic Leaktight Automata.” Leaktight class, Markov monoid, value-1 decidability ve PSPACE hattının ana kaynağı.
Günter Rote — “Probabilistic Finite Automaton Emptiness Is Undecidable for a Fixed Automaton” (MFCS 2025). Fixed automaton, variable starting distribution, positive/doubly-stochastic variants ve PCP reductions; CW-002H–M’nin merkezi contemporary collision’ı.
Mihir Vahanwala — “Skolem and Positivity Completeness of Ergodic Markov Chains.” LRS → ergodic Markov chain reduction, k↦k+1 state overhead ve stationary-threshold interpretation.
Gemma De les Coves, Joshua Graf, Andreas Klingler, Tim Netzer — “Positive Moments Forever: Undecidable and Decidable Cases.” Real-eigenvalue matrices ve real-root LRS positivity decidability; reversibility frontier’ını önemli biçimde düzeltti.
P. M. Gibson — “Products of symmetric doubly stochastic matrices over a field” (1976). Symmetric bistochastic factorization collision’ı.
Paul C. Bell — “Polynomially Ambiguous Probabilistic Automata on Restricted Languages” (2019). Commutative PFA matrices altında bile cutpoint undecidability’nin yaşayabildiğini gösteren collision.
Helmut Wielandt — “Unzerlegbare, nicht negative Matrizen” (1950). Primitive-matrix exponent ve (n−1)

G. Birkhoff — “Extensions of Jentzsch’s Theorem” (1957). Positive operators ve projective contraction temel kaynağı; Atar–Zeitouni bibliography’sinde de doğrudan yer alıyor.
