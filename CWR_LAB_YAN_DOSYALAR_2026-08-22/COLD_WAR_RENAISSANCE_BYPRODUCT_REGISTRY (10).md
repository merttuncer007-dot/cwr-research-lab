## Permanent rule

Every mathematically nontrivial byproduct generated by an Interaction Cell is archived as an independent research topic, even if:

- it does not contribute to DCSG;
- it contradicts the direction of the parent project;
- it is only an impossibility conjecture;
- it emerges while falsifying another idea;
- it appears to solve a historical problem outside the original target;
- it later turns out to be classical.

No topic is deleted. Its status changes.

### Status vocabulary

- SEED — precise question exists; little work done.
- ACTIVE — being investigated.
- EXACT-CANDIDATE — a falsifiable exact statement has been formulated.
- PROVED-PROVISIONALLY — self-contained proof exists; collision audit still open.
- CLASSICAL-COLLISION — result already known; retained as a useful bridge.
- PARTIAL — useful theorem/bound obtained, main problem still open.
- IMPOSSIBILITY — sharp obstruction proved.
- FALSIFIED — candidate killed; counterexample archived.
- DORMANT — currently low priority.
- OPEN-HISTORICAL — verified unresolved or incompletely resolved historical boundary.
- PROMOTE — sufficiently strong to become its own research program.

### Archive fields

Each topic records:
1. ID.

4. Exact research question.
5. Independent scientific value.

# CW-001 COLLISION HARVEST — Torgersen/Le Cam generalized-experiment line

The first serious collision search changed CW-001 immediately.

Torgersen's experiment theory explicitly introduces generalized variables to repair weak-compactness pathologies where pointwise limits of power functions need not be power functions of any ordinary test. His vector-lattice chapter then develops generalized experiments, transitions, M-spaces/L-spaces, and representation theory; later chapters represent experiments through standard/conical measures, with information ordering expressed by dilations and deficiency metrics tied to weak convergence.

Therefore CWR-BP-001 through CWR-BP-005 must now be split more carefully: the idea "complete ordinary experiments by larger objects" is not itself new. The open value lies in identifying which completion operation gives the exact common lower envelope and what the residual nonrepresentability/deficiency means.
